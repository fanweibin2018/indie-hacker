Talk to 30 strangers before you write a line of code写第一行代码前，先和 30 个陌生人聊聊

A repeatable interview script for validating demand without leading the witness — plus how to read the signal under the politeness, and the four questions that actually predict willingness to pay.一套可复用的需求验证访谈脚本：如何不诱导式提问，怎样从客气话里读出真实信号，以及真正能预测付费意愿的四个问题。

林Lin Yao林遥 2026.05.28 12 min 2,140 reads2,140 阅读
hero image · 21:9题图 · 21:9

Most failed indie products didn't fail at the code. They failed months earlier, the moment the founder decided the idea was good enough to build without ever testing whether anyone wanted it. The cheapest insurance you can buy is a conversation.大多数失败的独立产品，问题不在代码。它们在几个月前就已经失败了——就在创始人认定「这个点子够好，可以直接开干」、却从没验证过有没有人真正想要它的那一刻。你能买到的最便宜的保险，就是一次对话。

Over the last three launches I've standardized a 20-minute interview I run before committing to anything. The goal isn't to hear “yes, I'd use that.” Politeness is noise. The goal is to find evidence of existing behavior: what they already do, what it already costs them, and what they've already tried.在过去三次发布里，我把一套 20 分钟的访谈标准化了，凡是要投入的事，都先跑一遍。目标不是听到「会啊，我会用」——客气话是噪音。目标是找到既有行为的证据：他们现在已经在做什么、为此已经付出多少代价、已经试过哪些办法。

01Find the strangers找到这些陌生人

Your friends will lie to be nice. You need people who have the problem but no reason to flatter you. I source them where the pain is already being voiced out loud — niche subreddits, Discord servers, and the one-star reviews of adjacent products. A tool like PostScan surfaces the exact phrases people repeat, which doubles as your interview opener.朋友为了照顾面子会说假话。你需要的是真正有这个问题、却没有任何理由讨好你的人。我从痛点已经被大声说出来的地方找他们——细分的 subreddit、Discord 群，以及相邻产品的一星差评。像 PostScan 这样的工具能把人们反复念叨的原话抓出来，正好拿来当访谈的开场白。

“If they haven't already hacked together a workaround, you haven't found a real problem — you've found a nice-to-have.” 「如果他们还没有自己拼凑出一个临时方案，那你找到的就不是真问题，而是一个可有可无的『最好能有』。」 — the only filter that matters—— 唯一真正重要的筛子

02The script (don't lead the witness)访谈脚本（别诱导对方）

Every question is about the past, never the hypothetical future. “Would you pay for X?” is worthless. “Walk me through the last time you dealt with this” is gold. Keep a copy of the script open — AskKit has a template that bakes in the non-leading phrasing so you don't improvise your way into bias.每个问题都问过去，绝不问假设的未来。「你会为 X 付费吗？」一文不值；「带我回顾一下你上次处理这件事的经过」才是金矿。把脚本放在手边——AskKit 有一份模板，把「不诱导」的措辞固化进去，免得你即兴发挥、一不小心就把偏见带了进去。

  • Last time: When did you last run into this? Walk me through it.上一次：你上次碰到这个问题是什么时候？带我走一遍当时的经过。
  • Cost: What did that cost you — time, money, sanity?代价：它让你付出了什么——时间、金钱，还是心力？
  • Workaround: What do you do about it today?临时方案：你现在是怎么应付它的？
  • Spend: Have you ever paid to make it go away?已付费：你有没有为了摆脱它而花过钱？

03Turn talk into a number把对话变成一个数字

Interviews tell you what to build; they don't tell you how many people want it. Before I write code, I put up a fake-door landing page and route a tiny ad budget at it. If the click-to-signup rate clears my threshold, I build. If not, the conversations were lying to me politely. Here's the event I log on every waitlist submit:访谈告诉你该做什么，但不会告诉你有多少人想要。在写代码之前，我会架一个「假门」落地页，导一点点广告预算过去。如果「点击→注册」的转化率越过我的阈值，就开工；否则，说明那些对话只是在礼貌地骗我。下面是我在每次等候名单提交时记录的事件：

track.ts
// fire on waitlist submit — measure intent, not vanity
analytics.track("waitlist_joined", {
  variant:   "fake_door_v2",
  price_hint: "$12/mo",   // show price BEFORE the email
  source:    ctx.utm_source,
  ts:        Date.now(),
});

Show the price before you ask for the email. A signup that survives a real price tag is worth ten that don't. Spin the page up in minutes with Waitlisted and let the numbers — not your hopes — decide whether code gets written.在索要邮箱之前就把价格亮出来。一个扛得住真实价签的注册，抵得过十个扛不住的。用 Waitlisted 几分钟搭好页面，让数字——而不是你的一厢情愿——来决定要不要写代码。

Thirty conversations and one fake door will save you a quarter of wasted building. That's the whole trick. Everything after this is just execution.三十场对话加一扇假门，能帮你省下一个季度的无效开发。诀窍就这么多，剩下的全是执行。
