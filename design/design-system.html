Design system设计系统

Every color, type ramp, spacing step, radius, shadow and easing lives as a CSS custom property in tokens.css. The five phase colors share one chroma family and an even hue spread — they're a semantic code, not decoration.每一个颜色、字阶、间距、圆角、阴影与缓动都以 CSS 自定义属性的形式存放在 tokens.css 里。五个阶段色共享同一彩度族、色相均匀铺开——它们是语义色码，而非装饰。
