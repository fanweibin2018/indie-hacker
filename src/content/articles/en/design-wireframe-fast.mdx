Sketching key flows before writing code saves enormous rework. As an indie hacker you don't need designer-level polish — you just need clarity of intent.

## Two steps to fast wireframes

1. **Map the core flow**: Where does the user enter, what do they do, where do they end up? Keep each flow to five steps or fewer.
2. **Assemble from a UI kit**: The Figma Community has hundreds of free UI kits — drag-and-drop is 10× faster than drawing from scratch.

## Common mistakes

- Fussing over colors and fonts at wireframe stage — save that for the visual design pass.
- Trying to cover every edge case — wireframe only the happy path and handle the rest in code.

> A wireframe's job is to align understanding, not to replicate the final product.
