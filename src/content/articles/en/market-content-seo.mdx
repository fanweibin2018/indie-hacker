For indie hackers, SEO is one of the few "invest once, earn for years" growth channels. A single article that ranks on page one can keep sending traffic for years.

## Three actions to get started

1. **Keyword research**: Use Ahrefs or Google Search Console to find what your target users actually search for. Prioritize long-tail keywords with 100–1,000 monthly searches and low competition.
2. **Write problem-solving articles**: Don't write brand copy — genuinely answer the searcher's intent.
3. **Internal linking**: Link related articles to each other so search engines understand your site structure.

## Realistic expectations

SEO typically takes 3–6 months before you see meaningful results. Treat it as a long-term asset, not a quick-acquisition tactic.

> The best SEO content is "the article you wish you could have found three years ago."
